Illustrative matchup — opponent-adjusted efficiency model

An opponent-adjusted efficiency model for NCAA Tournament matchups

Every team's rating accounts for the strength of the opponents they actually played, not just their win-loss record — so a team that beat weak opponents doesn't rank ahead of one that beat strong ones. Ratings are computed using only games through Selection Sunday, so postseason results can never leak into the numbers used to project the postseason.

  • Opponent-adjusted efficiency margin, computed iteratively across a full season of Division I box scores
  • Backtested against real historical outcomes with a proper train/test split, scored on Brier score rather than raw accuracy alone
